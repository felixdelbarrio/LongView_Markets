# Portfolios

The real demo portfolio records buys, costs, fees and exposures. Backend summary reports total value, cost, unrealized return, estimated net return, positions and concentration.

## Operating Principle

Every financial page must expose source, date, data kind, confidence and limitations. Mock, simulation and forecast data are labeled separately from observed data.
